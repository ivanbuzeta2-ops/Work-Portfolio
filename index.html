Professional summary

Registered Civil Engineer (RCE) and Safety Officer II with over two years of experience in residential, commercial, industrial, and institutional roofing estimation.

I turn construction drawings and specifications into accurate quantity takeoffs and cost estimates, and keep project documentation organized so sales, engineering, and clients can move forward with confidence.

I have hands-on estimating experience in roofing systems, shear wall systems, and painting systems, working in PlanSwift, Bluebeam Revu, and STACK Construction Software. I am familiar with Xactimate and various CRM platforms, and I adapt quickly to new estimating and project management tools.

My strengths are attention to detail, organization, written communication, and time management. See Professional strengths for more.
